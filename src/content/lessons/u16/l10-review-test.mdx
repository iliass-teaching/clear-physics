| Topic | Key ideas |
|---|---|
| atom | small positive nucleus (protons +1, neutrons 0); electrons (−1) in orbit; ions: lose e⁻ → +, gain e⁻ → − |
| nuclide | ${}^{A}_{Z}\text{X}$: $Z$ protons, $A$ nucleons, $A - Z$ neutrons; isotopes: same $Z$, different $A$ |
| α-scattering (E) | mostly empty space; small, massive, positive nucleus |
| fission / fusion (E) | big nucleus splits / small nuclei join; mass decreases, energy released |
| background | radon, rocks and buildings, food and drink, cosmic rays; corrected count rate (E) |
| α, β, γ | α: He nucleus, very ionising, stopped by paper; β: electron, aluminium; γ: EM wave, lead |
| decay | spontaneous, random; α: $A-4$, $Z-2$; β: $Z+1$ (E) |
| half-life | time for half the nuclei to decay |
| safety | time, distance, shielding (E); tongs, lead boxes |
