<div class="table-wrap">

| Idea | Key facts |
|---|---|
| waves | transfer **energy**, not matter |
| transverse | vibration at **right angles** to the direction of travel: water waves, light, electromagnetic waves, S-waves |
| longitudinal | vibration **parallel** to the direction of travel: sound, P-waves (compressions and rarefactions) |
| parts of a wave | crest, trough, amplitude (middle line → crest), wavelength $\lambda$ (crest → next crest) |
| frequency | waves per second, in **Hz** |
| wave speed | $v = f\lambda$ |
| reflection | the wave comes back; same angle |
| refraction | change of speed (deep → shallow water: slower, shorter $\lambda$, same $f$) |
| diffraction | spreading through a gap or past an edge; most when gap ≈ $\lambda$ (Extended) |
