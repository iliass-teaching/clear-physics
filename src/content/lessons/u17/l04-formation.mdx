<Step kind="learn" title="Inner and outer planets" minutes={5}>

Compared to each other:

- the **four inner planets** (Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars) are **small** and **rocky**;
- the **four outer planets** (Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, Neptune) are **large** and made mostly of **gas**.

Why? The answer comes from the way the Solar System formed.

</Step>

<Step kind="learn" title="The accretion model" minutes={13}>

<Figure svg={accretionSvg()} caption="The accretion model in three steps (not to scale)." width="38rem" />

1. **A cloud of gas and dust.** About 4.6 billion years ago, there was a huge cloud of gas and dust in space. It contained hydrogen, helium and many other elements.
2. **Gravity pulls it together.** Gravity pulled the material of the cloud together. The cloud collapsed.
3. **It spins and flattens.** The cloud was rotating. As it collapsed, it spun faster and became a flat, spinning disc: an **accretion disc**.
4. **The Sun forms.** Most of the material went to the centre. It became very hot and dense, and formed the Sun.
5. **The planets form.** In the disc, small pieces collided and stuck together (this is **accretion**). They grew into planets.

### Why two kinds of planets?

- **Near the Sun**, it was very hot. Only rock and metal could stay solid. So the inner planets are **small and rocky**.
- **Far from the Sun**, it was cold. Ice could also stay solid, so there was more solid material. The planets grew bigger, and their strong gravity pulled in a lot of hydrogen and helium gas. So the outer planets are **large and made mostly of gas**.

</Step>

<Step kind="teacher" title="Teacher notes">

**Syllabus:** 6.1.2 C2 asks for the accretion model with (a) the dependence on gravity, (b) the presence of many elements in interstellar clouds of gas and dust, (c) the rotation of the material and the accretion disc. The temperature explanation for rocky and gas planets is the usual way to complete the model.

**Timing:** starter 7 min, learn 18 min, card sort 15 min, check 15 min, exit ticket 5 min.

**Common mistakes:**

- Thinking the planets formed first and the Sun later.
- Thinking the heavy material "fell" to the inside of the Solar System.

**Language support:**

- Show the word *accretion* with a snowball: it grows when it rolls and picks up snow.
- Spin a ball of pizza dough (or a video of it) to show how spinning makes a disc.

**Differentiation:**

- Support: give the first and the last cards of the story.
- Stretch: where did the elements heavier than helium in the cloud come from? (From older stars and supernovae: see Lesson 7.)

</Step>
