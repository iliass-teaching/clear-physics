<Step kind="learn" title="White light splits into colours" minutes={14}>

<Figure svg={prismSvg()} caption="A glass prism splits white light into a spectrum." width="32rem" />

- **White light** is a mixture of many colours.
- In glass, each colour travels at a slightly different speed, so each colour **refracts by a different amount**.
- **Violet** refracts the **most**. **Red** refracts the **least**.
- So a prism spreads white light into a band of colours: a **spectrum**. This is **dispersion**.

**The seven colours** (traditional), in order:

<div class="table-wrap">

| red | orange | yellow | green | blue | indigo | violet |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| longest wavelength | | | | | | shortest wavelength |
| lowest frequency | | | | | | highest frequency |

</div>

A memory sentence: **R**ichard **O**f **Y**ork **G**ave **B**attle **I**n **V**ain (ROYGBIV). You can also make your own.

**Extended:** light of **one single frequency** (one pure colour) is called **monochromatic** ("mono" = one, "chroma" = colour). A laser gives nearly monochromatic light.

</Step>

<Step kind="do" title="Make a spectrum" minutes={15}>

**Equipment:** a ray box with a single slit (white light), a triangular glass prism, white paper or a screen. Dim the room.

1. Shine the ray at one side of the prism.
2. Turn the prism slowly until you see a spectrum on the paper.
3. Draw the prism and the path of the light. Label the red and violet edges of the spectrum.

**No prism?** Put a glass of water in sunlight near a window, or look at a CD in bright light.

**Questions:**

1. Which colour is bent the most? Which the least?
2. Put a red filter in front of the ray box. What do you see after the prism now? Why?

</Step>

<Step kind="teacher" title="Teacher notes">

**Core and Extended:** dispersion and the seven colours (3.2.4 C1, C2) are Core. Monochromatic (S3) is Extended.

**Timing:** starter 5 min, learn 14 min, prism 15 min, check 16 min, exit ticket 5 min.

**Note:** the "seven colours" are traditional (Newton chose seven). The real spectrum is continuous; indigo is hard to see. The syllabus asks for the traditional seven.

**Common mistakes:**

- Reversing the order of frequency and wavelength.
- "The prism makes the colours."
- Thinking red refracts the most because it is "strongest".

**Language support:**

- *disperse* = spread out in different directions.
- *spectrum* (plural *spectra*).

**Differentiation:**

- Support: a colour strip to cut out and stick in order.
- Stretch: in the visible spectrum, how does the speed of light in glass change from red to violet?

**Safety:** do not use a laser without supervision; never look into a laser beam.

</Step>
