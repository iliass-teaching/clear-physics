Describe the motion of the car between 5 s and 15 s.

<ExamQuestion number="1(b)" marks={3}>

Calculate the total distance travelled by the car.

<div slot="scheme">

- distance = area under the graph **[1]**
- $\tfrac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 10 + 10 \times 10 + \tfrac{1}{2} \times 5 \times 10$ **[1]**

<ExamQuestion number="1(c) (E)" marks={2}>

Calculate the acceleration of the car in the first 5 s.

<div slot="scheme">

### Question 2: energy

<ExamQuestion number="2(a)" marks={2}>

A motor lifts a load. It does 1800 J of work in 12 s. Calculate the useful power output of the motor.

<div slot="scheme">

- $P = \dfrac{W}{t} = \dfrac{1800}{12}$ **[1]**
- $= 150\,\text{W}$ **[1]**

</div>
</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion number="2(b) (E)" marks={3}>

The electrical power input of the motor is 250 W. Calculate its efficiency, and state one reason why it is not 100%.

<div slot="scheme">

- efficiency $= \dfrac{150}{250} \times 100\%$ **[1]**
- $= 60\%$ **[1]**
- energy is transferred to the surroundings as thermal energy (friction / heating of wires) or sound **[1]**

</div>
</ExamQuestion>

### Question 3: thermal physics

<ExamQuestion number="3(a)" marks={3}>

Describe the arrangement, separation and motion of the particles in a liquid.

<div slot="scheme">

- arrangement: no regular pattern / random **[1]**
- separation: close together / touching **[1]**
- motion: move around each other / slide past each other **[1]**

</div>
</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion number="3(b)" marks={2}>

Explain why a metal spoon in hot tea feels hot at the handle, but a plastic spoon does not.

<div slot="scheme">

- metal is a good (thermal) conductor / has free electrons **[1]**
- plastic is a (thermal) insulator / poor conductor **[1]**

</div>
</ExamQuestion>

### Question 4: waves

<ExamQuestion number="4(a)" marks={3}>

A sound wave in air has a frequency of 500 Hz. The speed of sound in air is 340 m/s. Calculate the wavelength.

<div slot="scheme">

- $v = f\lambda$, so $\lambda = \dfrac{v}{f}$ **[1]**
- $= \dfrac{340}{500}$ **[1]**
- $= 0.68\,\text{m}$ **[1]**

</div>
</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion number="4(b)" marks={2}>

State two differences between a sound wave and a light wave.

<div slot="scheme">

Any two of:

- sound is longitudinal, light is transverse **[1]**
- sound needs a medium, light can travel through a vacuum **[1]**
- light is much faster (3.0 × 10⁸ m/s) than sound (about 340 m/s) **[1]**
- light is electromagnetic, sound is not **[1]**

</div>
</ExamQuestion>

### Question 5: electricity

<ExamQuestion number="5(a)" marks={3}>

A kettle is rated 230 V, 2300 W. Calculate the current in the kettle.

<div slot="scheme">

- $P = IV$, so $I = \dfrac{P}{V}$ **[1]**
- $= \dfrac{2300}{230}$ **[1]**
- $= 10\,\text{A}$ **[1]**

</div>
</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion number="5(b)" marks={3}>

The kettle has a metal case. Explain how the earth wire and the fuse protect the user if the live wire touches the case.

<div slot="scheme">

- a (large) current flows through the earth wire (to the ground) **[1]**
- the large current melts the fuse / the fuse blows **[1]**
- this disconnects the live wire, so the case is not live / the user does not get a shock **[1]**

</div>
</ExamQuestion>

### Question 6: radioactivity

<ExamQuestion number="6(a)" marks={2}>

The count rate from a sample falls from 800 counts/minute to 100 counts/minute in 24 hours. (Background is not included.) Determine the half-life.

<div slot="scheme">

- 800 → 400 → 200 → 100: three half-lives **[1]**
- half-life $= \dfrac{24}{3} = 8$ hours **[1]**

</div>
</ExamQuestion>

<ExamQuestion number="6(b)" marks={3}>

Suggest which type of radiation (α, β or γ) is used to sterilise medical equipment in sealed packets, and explain why.

<div slot="scheme">

- γ (gamma) **[1]**
- it is very penetrating / passes through the packet **[1]**
- it kills bacteria (and the equipment does not become radioactive) **[1]**

</div>
</ExamQuestion>
