**Mistakes to avoid:**

- ammeter in parallel or voltmeter in series;
- "the current is used up" in a lamp;
- adding resistances in parallel;
- fuse rating smaller than the normal current;
- confusing the motor effect (current → force) and induction (motion → e.m.f.);
- turns ratio of a transformer upside down.
