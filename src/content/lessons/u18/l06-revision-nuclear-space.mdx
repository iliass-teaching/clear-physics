**Mistakes to avoid:**

- "γ is the most ionising" (α is the most ionising; γ is the most penetrating);
- half-life: dividing by the number of half-lives instead of halving again and again;
- β-decay: $Z$ goes **up** by 1 (E);
- seasons explained by the distance from the Sun (the reason is the **tilt** of the axis);
- the light-year is a **distance**, not a time.
