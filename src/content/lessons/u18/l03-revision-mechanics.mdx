**Mistakes to avoid:**

- mass (kg) and weight (N) mixed up;
- speed–time graph: the **area** is the distance, the **gradient** is the acceleration;
- moments: the distance must be **perpendicular** to the force, measured from the **pivot**;
- energy: "energy is used up" (it is transferred, never destroyed);
- pressure: cm² not converted to m².
