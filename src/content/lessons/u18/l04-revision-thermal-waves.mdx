**Mistakes to avoid:**

- "particles expand" (the spaces between them change);
- "heat rises" (hot fluid rises because it is less dense);
- mixing up evaporation (any temperature, surface) and boiling;
- angles of incidence and reflection measured from the surface (use the normal);
- the order of the EM spectrum (radio, micro, infrared, visible, UV, X-rays, gamma);
- forgetting to halve the distance in echo questions.
