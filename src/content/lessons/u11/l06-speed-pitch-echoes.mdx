<Step kind="learn" title="Measuring the speed of sound" minutes={10}>

<Figure svg={speedOfSoundSvg()} caption="Measuring the speed of sound outdoors." width="32rem" />

1. Measure a long distance (for example 200 m) with a measuring tape.
2. Student A bangs two wooden blocks together (or uses a starting pistol).
3. Student B **sees** the blocks hit and starts the stopwatch. The light arrives almost at once.
4. Student B **hears** the sound and stops the stopwatch.
5. speed $= \dfrac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}$. Repeat several times and use the mean time.

**Why a long distance?** The time is longer, so the reaction time of student B is a smaller part of it: the result is more accurate.

**Echo method:** stand far in front of a large wall. Clap, and time the echo (the sound travels to the wall **and back**: distance = $2d$).

</Step>

<Step kind="learn" title="Loudness and pitch" minutes={10}>

A microphone and an **oscilloscope** show a sound as a wave on a screen.

<Figure svg={soundTracesSvg()} caption="Four sounds on an oscilloscope screen." width="32rem" />

- **Bigger amplitude** → **louder** sound.
- **Higher frequency** (more waves on the screen) → **higher pitch**.

</Step>

<Step kind="learn" title="Echoes" minutes={5}>

<Figure svg={echoSvg()} caption="An echo is sound reflected from a surface." width="30rem" />

An **echo** is the **reflection** of a sound wave from a hard surface (a wall, a cliff). Soft materials (curtains, carpets) absorb sound and reduce echoes.

<Step kind="teacher" title="Teacher notes">

**Timing:** starter 5 min, speed 10 min, loudness and pitch 10 min, oscilloscope 12 min, echoes 5 min, practice 13 min, exit ticket 5 min.

**About the simulation:** the sound uses the Web Audio API of the browser. It plays only after a click and stops after 3 seconds. Keep the volume low.

**Common mistakes:**

- "Louder sounds travel faster." The speed does not depend on loudness.
- "Higher pitch = bigger amplitude."
- Forgetting to halve the distance in echo questions.

**Language support:**

- *pitch* = high or low (like a bird or a lion); *loudness* = loud or quiet.

**Differentiation:**

- Support: level 1 and 2 of the practice only.
- Stretch: suggest two ways to make the outdoor measurement more accurate.

**Safety:** starting pistols only with school permission; never point them at people. Choose a safe open space.

</Step>
