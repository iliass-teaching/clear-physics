<Step kind="learn" title="Uses" minutes={15}>

<div class="table-wrap">

| Region | Typical uses |
|---|---|
| **radio waves** | radio and television transmissions; astronomy; RFID (contactless cards, tags in shops) |
| **microwaves** | satellite television; mobile phones (cell phones); microwave ovens |
| **infrared** | electric grills; TV remote controls; intruder alarms; thermal imaging; optical fibres |
| **visible light** | vision; photography; illumination (lighting) |
| **ultraviolet** | security marking; detecting fake bank notes; sterilising water |
| **X-rays** | medical scanning (bones); security scanners |
| **gamma rays** | sterilising food and medical equipment; detecting and treating cancer |

</div>

<Step kind="learn" title="Dangers" minutes={10}>

Too much exposure to some electromagnetic waves is harmful:

<div class="table-wrap">

| Region | Harmful effect |
|---|---|
| **microwaves** | internal heating of body cells |
| **infrared** | skin burns |
| **ultraviolet** | damage to surface cells and eyes: can lead to skin cancer and eye conditions |
| **X-rays** and **gamma rays** | mutation or damage to cells in the body |

</div>

**In general:** the **higher the frequency**, the more energy each wave carries, and the more dangerous it is. X-rays and gamma rays can go deep into the body and damage cells.

**Protection:** sunscreen and sunglasses (UV); short X-ray exposures, and lead aprons for staff (X-rays); the metal box of a microwave oven keeps the microwaves inside.

</Step>

<Step kind="do" title="See the invisible" minutes={13}>

1. **Infrared:** point a TV remote control at the camera of a mobile phone (many phone cameras can detect infrared; the front camera often works best). Press a button. What do you see on the screen?
2. **Ultraviolet:** write a secret message with a UV security pen. Shine a UV lamp on it. (Do not look at the UV lamp.)
3. **Card sort:** match 14 cards (7 regions, 7 uses or dangers).

</Step>

<Step kind="teacher" title="Teacher notes">

**Timing:** starter 5 min, uses 15 min, dangers 10 min, activities 13 min, check 12 min, exit ticket 5 min.

**Syllabus:** the lists of uses and dangers follow 3.3 C3 and C4. Students should know at least these examples.

**Common mistakes:**

- Mixing up the uses of UV and X-rays.
- "Radio waves are sound waves." Radio waves are electromagnetic; a radio turns them into sound.
- "All radiation is dangerous." Low-frequency radiation (radio, visible) is not dangerous in normal amounts.

**Language support:**

- *sterilise* = kill all bacteria; *mutation* = a change in the DNA of a cell; *RFID* = radio frequency identification.

**Differentiation:**

- Support: the card sort with pictures.
- Stretch: why are gamma rays good for sterilising food that is already in sealed packets?

**Safety:** do not look directly at UV lamps; use low-power UV torches.

</Step>
