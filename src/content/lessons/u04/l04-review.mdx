For each question, write the equation you need (do not calculate yet):

1. A 5 kg ball moves at 2 m/s. Find its momentum.
2. A force of 30 N acts for 0.1 s. Find the impulse.
3. A car’s momentum changes by 6000 kg m/s in 0.2 s. Find the force.
4. Two trolleys collide and stick. Find their velocity after.

<Step kind="learn" title="Unit 4 in one page" minutes={10}>

<div class="table-wrap">

| Idea | Equation | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| momentum | $p = mv$ | kg m/s |
| impulse | $F\Delta t = \Delta(mv)$ | N s |
| force | $F = \dfrac{\Delta p}{\Delta t}$ | N |
| conservation of momentum | total momentum before = total momentum after | |

</div>

**Method for momentum problems:**

1. Choose a positive direction (usually to the right).
2. Give each velocity a sign: $+$ to the right, $-$ to the left.
3. Write "momentum before = momentum after".
4. Solve. Give the size **and** the direction of your answer.

</Step>
