<Step kind="learn" title="What is power?" minutes={12}>

**Power** is how fast energy is transferred: the work done (or energy transferred) **per unit time**.

$$
P = \frac{W}{t} \qquad\qquad P = \frac{\Delta E}{t}
$$

<div class="table-wrap">

| Quantity | Symbol | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| power | $P$ | watt (W) |
| work done | $W$ | J |
| energy transferred | $\Delta E$ | J |
| time | $t$ | s |

</div>

$1\,\text{W} = 1\,\text{J/s}$: a power of 1 W transfers 1 J every second. Also $1\,\text{kW} = 1000\,\text{W}$ and $1\,\text{MW} = 1\,000\,000\,\text{W}$.

In the starter, the work done is the same, but running does it in less time, so your **power** is bigger.

<div class="table-wrap">

| device | typical power |
|---|---|
| LED lamp | 5–10 W |
| laptop | 50 W |
| microwave oven | 800 W |
| kettle | 2–3 kW |
| car engine | 50–150 kW |

</div>

<WorkedExample
  question="A kettle transfers 180 000 J of energy in 60 s. Calculate its power."
  given={['$\\Delta E = 180\\,000\\,\\text{J}$', '$t = 60\\,\\text{s}$']}
  find="the power $P$"
  answer="$P = 3000\,\text{W} = 3\,\text{kW}$"
>

$P = \dfrac{\Delta E}{t} = \dfrac{180\,000}{60}$

$P = 3000\,\text{W}$

</WorkedExample>

</Step>

<Step kind="do" title="How powerful are you?" minutes={18}>

Work in groups of 3, on a staircase. **Equipment:** bathroom scales (or use your mass if you know it), tape measure, stopwatch.

1. Measure the height of one step. Count the steps. Calculate the total height $\Delta h$.
2. Find your mass $m$ in kg.
3. Walk up the stairs. Your partner times you. Then run up safely, and time again.
4. Calculate the work done: $W = mg\Delta h$.
5. Calculate your power for walking and for running: $P = \dfrac{W}{t}$.

<Step kind="teacher" title="Teacher notes">

**Timing:** starter 5 min, learn 12 min, stairs 18 min, practice 20 min, exit ticket 5 min.

**Common mistakes:**

- Forgetting to change kW to W, or minutes to seconds.
- Confusing power (W) and energy (J).
- Using the mass instead of the weight in $W = mg\Delta h$.

**Device powers:** typical values only; check the labels of your own devices.

**Language support:**

- In everyday English, *powerful* can mean strong. In physics, power means how fast energy is transferred.

**Differentiation:**

- Support: give the height of the stairs.
- Stretch: an electric car has a 150 kW motor. How long would it take to transfer the energy in a 60 kWh battery at full power? (24 minutes.)

**Safety:** students with health problems can time others instead of running. Run up the stairs only, carefully, with a clear staircase; walk down.

</Step>
