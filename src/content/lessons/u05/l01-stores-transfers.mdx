<Step kind="learn" title="Energy stores" minutes={12}>

Energy is measured in **joules (J)**. Energy is kept in **stores**:

<div class="table-wrap">

| Energy store | The energy is in… | Example |
|---|---|---|
| kinetic | a moving object | a running child, a moving car |
| gravitational potential | an object raised up high | water behind a dam, a book on a shelf |
| chemical | fuels, food, batteries | petrol, bread, a phone battery |
| elastic (strain) | a stretched or squashed object | a stretched spring, a bow ready to shoot |
| nuclear | the nucleus of atoms | uranium in a nuclear power station |
| electrostatic | charges that attract or repel | a charged balloon, a thundercloud |
| internal (thermal) | the particles of a hot object | a cup of hot tea |

</div>

</Step>

<Step kind="learn" title="Energy transfers" minutes={10}>

Energy moves from one store to another. There are four ways:

1. by **forces**: mechanical work done (a push or a pull moves something);
2. by **electrical currents**: electrical work done;
3. by **heating**: from a hotter object to a colder one;
4. by **waves**: electromagnetic waves (like light) and sound.

<Figure svg={flowSvg(['chemical store|(battery)', 'electrical|work done', 'light + heating|the surroundings'])} caption="A torch: energy moves from the battery to the surroundings." width="34rem" />

More examples:

- A ball falls: gravitational potential store → kinetic store (by the force of gravity).
- A kettle: electrical work → internal (thermal) store of the water.
- A bow shoots an arrow: elastic store → kinetic store of the arrow.

</Step>

<Step kind="teacher" title="Teacher notes">

**Timing:** starter 5 min, learn 22 min, energy detectives 15 min, check 13 min, exit ticket 5 min.

**Common mistakes:**

- Calling a way of transfer a store ("light energy", "sound energy", "electrical energy"). The syllabus uses stores and transfers: light and sound are waves that transfer energy; electrical work is a transfer.
- Saying energy is "used up" or "lost". It is transferred, often to the surroundings.

**Energy detectives answers (main transfers):** car brakes: kinetic → internal (thermal) of brakes and surroundings, by forces (friction). Phone: chemical (battery) → electrical work → sound waves + heating. Firework: chemical → light, sound, kinetic, internal. Wind-up toy: elastic → kinetic (by forces). Log: chemical → heating + light. Skier: gravitational potential → kinetic (+ internal by friction).

**Language support:**

- Keep the 7 stores and 4 transfers on the wall for the whole unit.

**Differentiation:**

- Support: give the stores on cards to choose from.
- Stretch: describe the energy transfers in a hydroelectric power station.

</Step>
