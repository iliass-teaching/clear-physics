<Step kind="learn" title="What is efficiency?" minutes={10}>

No device transfers all its energy usefully: some is always **wasted**, usually by heating the surroundings.

**Efficiency** tells us how much of the energy put in is transferred **usefully**. A more efficient device wastes less energy.

<Step kind="learn" title="Calculating efficiency (Extended)" minutes={12}>

$$
\text{efficiency} = \frac{\text{useful energy output}}{\text{total energy input}} \times 100\%
$$

$$
\text{efficiency} = \frac{\text{useful power output}}{\text{total power input}} \times 100\%
$$

Efficiency has **no unit**. It is a number less than 1, or a percentage less than 100%.

<WorkedExample
  question="An electric motor takes in 800 J. It does 600 J of useful work lifting a load. Calculate its efficiency."
  given={['total energy input $= 800\\,\\text{J}$', 'useful energy output $= 600\\,\\text{J}$']}
  find="the efficiency"
  answer="$75\%$"
>

$\text{efficiency} = \dfrac{600}{800} \times 100\%$

$\text{efficiency} = 0.75 \times 100\% = 75\%$

</WorkedExample>

**No device can be more than 100% efficient**: that would mean creating energy.

</Step>

<Step kind="learn" title="Improving efficiency" minutes={5}>

To make a device more efficient, reduce the wasted energy:

- **lubricate** moving parts (less friction, less heating);
- **insulate** (a kettle or an oven loses less energy by heating the surroundings);
- use **better designs** (LED lamps instead of filament lamps; streamlined cars).

</Step>

<Step kind="teacher" title="Teacher notes">

**Core and Extended:** the idea of efficiency (1.7.3 C3) is Core. The two equations (1.7.3 S7) are Extended.

**Timing:** starter 5 min, learn 27 min, practice 20 min, exit ticket 8 min.

**Common mistakes:**

- Dividing the wrong way (input ÷ output gives more than 100%).
- Using the wasted energy instead of the useful energy.
- Giving efficiency a unit (J or W).

**Data note:** lamp efficiencies are approximate. Filament lamps transfer roughly 5–10% of their energy as light; LEDs much more.

**Safety (starter):** filament lamps get very hot. Students should hold a hand near the lamp, not touch the glass.

**Language support:**

- Sentence frame: *"The … is more efficient because it wastes less energy by …"*

**Differentiation:**

- Support: compare two Sankey diagrams and say which device is more efficient.
- Stretch: a power station is 35% efficient. It gives 700 MW of electrical power. What is its input power? (2000 MW.)

</Step>
