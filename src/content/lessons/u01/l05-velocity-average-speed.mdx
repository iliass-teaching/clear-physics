<Step kind="learn" title="Velocity" minutes={8}>

**Velocity** is speed in a given direction.

- speed: 20 m/s (a scalar)
- velocity: 20 m/s **to the north** (a vector)

In the starter, the two cars have the same speed but **different velocities**.

A car goes around a roundabout at a constant speed. Its direction changes all the time, so its velocity changes too.

</Step>

<Step kind="learn" title="Average speed" minutes={12}>

On a real journey, the speed changes: we speed up, slow down and stop.
The **average speed** is for the whole journey:

$$
\text{average speed} = \frac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}
$$

<WorkedExample
  question="A bus goes 1200 m in 300 s. It stops for 120 s. Then it goes 2400 m in 480 s."
  given={['part 1: $1200\\,\\text{m}$ in $300\\,\\text{s}$', 'stop: $120\\,\\text{s}$', 'part 2: $2400\\,\\text{m}$ in $480\\,\\text{s}$']}
  find="the average speed for the whole journey"
  answer="$4\,\text{m/s}$"
>

$\text{total distance} = 1200 + 2400 = 3600\,\text{m}$

$\text{total time} = 300 + 120 + 480 = 900\,\text{s}$ (include the stop)

$\text{average speed} = \dfrac{3600}{900} = 4\,\text{m/s}$

</WorkedExample>

**Be careful:** do not take the mean of the speeds.

Example: a cyclist rides 1000 m at 10 m/s (100 s), then 1000 m at 20 m/s (50 s).

- Wrong: $\dfrac{10 + 20}{2} = 15\,\text{m/s}$.
- Right: $\text{average speed} = \dfrac{2000}{150} = 13.3\,\text{m/s}$.

The cyclist spends more time at the slow speed, so the average is closer to 10 m/s.

</Step>

<Step kind="teacher" title="Teacher notes">

**Timing:** starter 5 min, learn 20 min, task 15 min, practice 15 min, exit ticket 5 min.

**Common mistakes:**

- Taking the mean of two speeds instead of total distance ÷ total time.
- Forgetting the time spent stopped.
- Saying velocity is "just a more scientific word for speed".

**Answers for Lin's journey:** part 1: 1.3 m/s; part 3: 8.3 m/s; part 4: 2 m/s. Average: $\dfrac{3600}{1000} = 3.6\,\text{m/s}$.

**Language support:**

- Sentence frame: *"The speed is …, but the velocity is … to the …"*
- Point to a map on the wall: "to the north", "to the east".

**Differentiation:**

- Support: start with journeys without a stop.
- Stretch: a car travels 30 km at 60 km/h, then 30 km at 30 km/h. Find the average speed. (Answer: 40 km/h, not 45 km/h.)

</Step>
