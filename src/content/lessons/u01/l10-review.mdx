<Step kind="learn" title="Unit 1 in one page" minutes={10}>

<div class="table-wrap">

| Idea | Equation or rule | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| speed | $v = \dfrac{s}{t}$ | m/s |
| average speed | $\dfrac{\text{total distance}}{\text{total time}}$ | m/s |
| acceleration (Extended) | $a = \dfrac{\Delta v}{\Delta t}$ | m/s² |
| free fall | $g \approx 9.8\,\text{m/s}^2$, constant near the Earth | m/s² |
| small measurements | measure many, then divide | |
| vectors at right angles (Extended) | Pythagoras for the size, tan for the direction | |

</div>

**Graph rules:**

- Distance–time graph: **gradient = speed**. Flat line = at rest.
- Speed–time graph: **gradient = acceleration** (Extended). **Area = distance**. Flat line = constant speed.

</Step>

<Step kind="learn" title="Command words: what kind of answer?" minutes={10}>

<div class="table-wrap">

| Command word | Example question | Example answer |
|---|---|---|
| **State** | State the unit of acceleration. | m/s² |
| **Describe** | Describe the motion in part B of a speed–time graph (a flat line at 8 m/s). | The bus moves at a constant speed of 8 m/s. |
| **Explain** | Explain why a skydiver reaches terminal velocity. | As the speed increases, air resistance increases. When air resistance is equal to the weight, the forces balance. So the acceleration is zero and the speed is constant. |
| **Calculate** | Calculate the speed of a car that travels 600 m in 30 s. | $v = \dfrac{s}{t} = \dfrac{600}{30} = 20\,\text{m/s}$ |

</div>

- **State:** short answer, no reason.
- **Describe:** say *what* happens.
- **Explain:** say *why* it happens. Use "because" or "so".
- **Calculate:** write the equation, put in the numbers, give the answer **with the unit**.

<Step kind="teacher" title="Teacher notes">

**Timing:** starter 5 min, review 20 min, mixed practice 10 min, unit test 25 min.

**Using the test:** questions 1, 2, 4–8 test Core content; 3, 9 and 10 are Extended. Ask students to note their wrong answers: the feedback for each wrong option names the mistake, which makes a quick class analysis possible.

**Command words:** the full list, explained in clear English, is on the Key words page. Practise one or two command words in each later unit.

**Differentiation:**

- Support: students can use the "Unit 1 in one page" table during the test the first time.
- Stretch: ask students to write one exam-style question for each command word, with a model answer.

</Step>
