<Step kind="learn" title="What is speed?" minutes={15}>

**Speed** tells us how far something moves in one second.

To find the speed, divide the distance by the time:

$$
\text{speed} = \frac{\text{distance}}{\text{time}}
\qquad\text{or}\qquad
v = \frac{s}{t}
$$

<div class="table-wrap">

| Quantity | Symbol | Unit |
|---|---|---|
| speed | $v$ | m/s (metres per second) |
| distance | $s$ | m (metres) |
| time | $t$ | s (seconds) |

</div>

**Be careful:** in physics, $s$ is the symbol for **distance**. But the unit s means **seconds**.

### Back to the starter

- Ana: $v = \dfrac{100}{20} = 5\,\text{m/s}$. She runs 5 m in each second.
- Ben: $v = \dfrac{150}{25} = 6\,\text{m/s}$. He runs 6 m in each second.

So **Ben is faster**.

### Find the distance or the time

We can change the equation to find $s$ or $t$. Start from:

$$
v = \frac{s}{t}
$$

**To find the distance:** multiply both sides by $t$.

$$
v \times t = s \qquad\text{so}\qquad s = v \times t
$$

**To find the time:** start from $s = v \times t$. Divide both sides by $v$.

$$
\frac{s}{v} = t \qquad\text{so}\qquad t = \frac{s}{v}
$$

### Units of speed

We can write speed in **m/s**, **km/h** or **cm/s**.
To change km/h to m/s:

1. $1\,\text{km} = 1000\,\text{m}$
2. $1\,\text{h} = 3600\,\text{s}$
3. So $1\,\text{km/h} = \dfrac{1000}{3600}\,\text{m/s}$. This is the same as dividing by 3.6.

Example: $72\,\text{km/h} = \dfrac{72}{3.6} = 20\,\text{m/s}$.

</Step>

<Step kind="teacher" title="Teacher notes">

**Timing:** starter 5 min, learn 15 min, do 15 min, practice 15 min, exit ticket 5 min.

**Common mistakes** (the online practice detects them and gives a hint):

- Multiplying $s \times t$, or dividing $\dfrac{t}{s}$.
- Confusing the symbol $s$ (distance) with the unit s (seconds).
- Forgetting the unit, or mixing km with s, or m with minutes.
- From km/h to m/s: multiplying by 3.6 instead of dividing.

**Language support:**

- Put the key words on the wall, each with a picture.
- Read the starter aloud. Let students discuss in pairs before they write.
- Give a sentence frame: *"The speed of the … is … m/s. It moves … m in each second."*

**Differentiation:**

- Support: give the three forms of the equation on a card ($v = \dfrac{s}{t}$, $s = v \times t$, $t = \dfrac{s}{v}$), and practise the rearranging step together. Use Level 1 numbers that divide exactly.
- Stretch: compare animal speeds (cheetah, human, snail) in m/s and in km/h. Ask why the measured walking speed is not exact (reaction time).

**Safety:** clear the corridor before the walking practical. Walk, do not run.

**Next lesson:** velocity and average speed (1.2 C2, C3).

</Step>
