<Step kind="learn" title="Answering 'explain' questions" minutes={8}>

**Question:** *Explain why a car moving at a constant speed on a straight road needs a driving force.*

**Weak answer:** "Because it is moving."

**Strong answer:** "Friction and air resistance act backwards on the car. The driving force balances them. So the resultant force is zero and the velocity stays constant."

A strong "explain" answer:

1. names the forces;
2. compares them (balanced or unbalanced, which is bigger);
3. gives the result, with **so** or **because**.

**Your turn:** explain why a cyclist slows down when she stops pedalling.

</Step>

<Step kind="teacher" title="Teacher notes">

**Timing:** starter 5 min, review 18 min, mixed practice 12 min, unit test 25 min.

**Starter answers:** 1. mass (not a force). 2. kg (not a unit linked to force). 3. weigh (not a change of shape).

**"Your turn" model answer:** "When she stops pedalling, there is no driving force. Friction and air resistance act backwards. So the resultant force is backwards and she slows down."

**Using the test:** questions 1, 2, 4, 5, 7, 9 and 10 test Core content; 3, 6 and 8 are Extended. The feedback for each wrong answer names the mistake.

**Differentiation:**

- Support: allow the "Unit 3 in one page" table during the test the first time.
