| Idea | Equation or rule | Units |
|---|---|---|
| pressure | $p = \dfrac{F}{A}$ | N/m² (Pa) or N/cm² |
| small area | big pressure (knife, nail, studs) | |
| big area | small pressure (snowshoes, tractor tyres) | |
| liquids | pressure increases with depth and with density | |
| liquids (Extended) | $\Delta p = \rho g \Delta h$ | Pa |

</div>

**Units to watch:** $1\,\text{m}^2 = 10\,000\,\text{cm}^2$. $1\,\text{Pa} = 1\,\text{N/m}^2$. Density in kg/m³ for $\Delta p = \rho g \Delta h$.
